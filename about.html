OUR STORY // FROM THE CLASSROOM TO THE UNDERGROUND

4º ESO COMPULSORY SECONDARY EDUCATION

Welcome to Supersalchicha Music Agency!

We are Daniel Barceló, Álvaro Pinar and Alejandro Canet, three students currently studying 4th year of ESO (Compulsory Secondary Education).

Supersalchicha Music Agency is a project we have created as part of our schoolwork. Our goal is to design and present our own MUSIC agency, inspired by the UNDERGROUND electronic music scene, especially TRAP AND TECHNO.

Throughout this project, we have worked on creating our own music agency, developing its identity, designing its website and imagining how we would organise music festivals and events.

We have put a lot of effort, CREATIVE thinking and enthusiasm into making this project as REALISTIC AND PROFESSIONAL as possible.

Although this is a school project, we wanted to create something that looks and feels like a real music agency.

Thank you for visiting our website and taking the time to discover our project. We hope you enjoy it as much as we enjoyed creating it!
